Go Nomads

The Road Needs More Gatherings.

Go Nomads is the community gathering arm of Tribes of Nomad.

Arrive as strangers. Leave as a tribe.
Made to be joined

Everyone becomes part of the experience.

We bring travellers, backpackers, creators and curious humans together through experiences designed around participation rather than spectatorship.

This is not about standing around watching something happen.

Everyone becomes part of the experience.

One gathering might begin with strangers arriving with backpacks and tents.

Then come conversations.

Music.

Drum circles.

Beach mornings.

Games.

Shared experiences.

And somewhere in between, a group of people starts feeling like a tribe.

"There is no audience here. Bring your energy, curiosity and the version of yourself that is ready to join in."
What happens at Go Nomads?

A little bit planned. A lot shared.
